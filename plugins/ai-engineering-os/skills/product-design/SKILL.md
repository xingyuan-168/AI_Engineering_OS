# Product design

Read the approved scope, requirements, constraints, and research evidence. Define actors, journeys, information structure, feature boundaries, edge states, accessibility goals, and measurable acceptance criteria. Map each design decision back to a requirement or identify it explicitly as an assumption requiring approval.

Keep the canonical design in the task's allowed documentation paths. Surface conflicts between user value, feasibility, privacy, and operational constraints instead of silently choosing. Hand off unresolved questions, source references, artifact hashes, and the decisions that interaction, UI, architecture, and QA must preserve.
