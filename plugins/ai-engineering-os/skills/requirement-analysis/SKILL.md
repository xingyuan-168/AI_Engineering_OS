# Requirement analysis

Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, open questions, exclusions, and risks. Preserve the user's language for business rules while rewriting acceptance criteria so they are observable and testable.

For intake, update `docs/PROJECT_MASTER.md` and `docs/SCOPE.md` with the goal, users, measurable success, boundaries, dependencies, and unresolved decisions. For detailed requirements, update `docs/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `docs/USER_STORY.md`, `docs/BUSINESS_RULES.md`, and `docs/SCOPE.md` with stable IDs and acceptance criteria.

Do not silently resolve a question that materially changes scope, compliance, data ownership, or release risk. Surface it for the next approval gate. Check document links and required headings before handing off, then report artifact hashes and verification evidence to the orchestrator.
